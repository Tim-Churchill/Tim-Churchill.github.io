About / Boulder, Colorado

I like hard problems with moving parts.

I graduated from the Colorado School of Mines and work across computer science and robotics. My projects span autonomous flight, adaptive mechanisms, computer vision, and tools for making complex knowledge visible.

I care about the full path from an idea to dependable behavior: model the system, build the prototype, measure what actually happens, then make the next version clearer and better.
